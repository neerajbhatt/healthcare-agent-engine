## Why This Matters (0:20 – 0:50)

“Healthcare fraud detection is fundamentally a relationship problem.

Traditional rule-based systems often struggle to identify hidden behavioral patterns across providers, patients, procedures, claims, and billing relationships.

This platform is being designed to support intelligent anomaly detection using graph relationships, behavioral signals, and explainable AI techniques.

But equally important is the engineering process itself.

The real innovation here is using AI-assisted workflows to accelerate product definition, architecture thinking, and system decomposition before development even begins.”

## BMAD Workflow Introduction (0:50 – 1:30)

“Instead of jumping directly into implementation, I am using the BMAD workflow to create structured product artifacts.

This creates consistency and traceability across the engineering lifecycle.

For leadership teams, this is important because it reduces ambiguity early in the process.

It also enables faster alignment between business objectives, architecture strategy, and engineering execution.”

## Product Vision (1:30 – 2:20)

“The platform being defined here is a multi-agent fraud intelligence system.

The vision is to allow investigators and analysts to interact with fraud patterns using natural language queries.

Under the hood, specialized AI-driven workflows can eventually collaborate across multiple dimensions of fraud analysis.

For example:

provider behavior analysis,

claims pattern correlation,

patient-service relationship analysis,

anomaly scoring,

and explainability generation.

The long-term goal is to create an intelligent investigation environment instead of a static reporting dashboard.”

“At this stage, I am intentionally focusing on product specification before writing production code.

For enterprise environments, this can significantly reduce downstream rework and improve architectural clarity before implementation investment begins.”
